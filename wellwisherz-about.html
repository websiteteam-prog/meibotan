About WellWisherz

We are the well-wisher your parents deserve.

Not a hospital. Not a nursing home. Just trained, trusted, warm human companions who show up — on time, in uniform, with care.

Our Story

WellWisherz is the first dedicated senior companionship service serving Chandigarh and Panchkula — a trained squad of care professionals who spend meaningful time with your parents so they never feel alone, forgotten, or stuck.

Our companions are background-verified, soft-skills trained, and uniformed — so your family knows exactly who is walking through that door. Every visit is tracked, reported, and followed up on WhatsApp so you’re always in the loop.

We handle the mundane and the meaningful: the prescription run, the morning walk, the temple visit, the phone call that takes 20 minutes to navigate, the evening chess game. Everything a well-wisher does.

Your parents don’t need pity — they need company.

They need someone who listens to stories from 40 years ago with genuine interest. Someone who helps them feel capable, engaged, and valued.

We’ve designed every touchpoint — the booking, the visit, the follow-up — around what families like yours actually need: reliability, transparency, and the peace of knowing someone is there.

Starting at just ₹1,600 per month for a Basic plan, WellWisherz is affordable enough to sustain, meaningful enough to transform a senior’s daily life.
