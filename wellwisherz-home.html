Why it matters

The quiet crisis in our homes.

Children move to different cities and countries. Nuclear families shrink. And the parents who spent decades building lives around their children now find themselves spending entire days without a single meaningful conversation.

Loneliness in seniors isn’t just an emotional problem — research links it to cognitive decline, depression, poor medication compliance, and a dramatically higher risk of hospitalisation. A caring companion isn’t a luxury. It’s healthcare.

WellWisherz exists because someone should be there — for the doctor visit you couldn’t attend, for the conversation over evening tea, for the errand that needed running. We do everything a well-wisher would do.
